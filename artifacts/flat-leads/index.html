    How this updates

    A Claude routine runs daily. It finds new Pune rental groups, reads the newest posts in the best ones, keeps the posts offering a flat or room, and republishes this page. It never posts, comments or messages anyone.

    It reads Facebook as the account in the FB_COOKIES_JSON variable of the Claude cloud environment. If the status says the session expired, export fresh cookies and replace that variable.

    Shortlisted, contacted and dismissed marks are saved for everyone who opens this page.
